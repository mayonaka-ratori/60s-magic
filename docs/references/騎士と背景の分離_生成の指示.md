Consistent cool overcast lighting, photographic realistic game render, no style change, do not simplify the detail.
Cell 1 left: standing guard, exact original neutral stance, sword tip down and out to viewer-left, shield beside body.
Cell 2 center: hit-reaction pose, his chest and shoulders recoil backwards and slightly to viewer-right, head flinches, sword arm drops a little, shield tilts outward. Feet still grounded at same baseline, no walking, no fall. A readable restrained flinch.
Cell 3 right: recovering guard pose, torso leaning slightly forward, shield raised across upper torso a little, sword hand closer to body. Same feet and scale, same design.
Characters must be independent cutouts against actual transparency. All swords, shield edges, horns, boots entirely within their own cells. No glow or impact effects baked into the image. These three sprites will be animated over the original game background.
